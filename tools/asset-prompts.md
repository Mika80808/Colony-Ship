# Imagegen prompts

All artwork was generated using the built-in imagegen tool, followed by local alpha extraction and sprite normalization as authorized by the user. Final assets are in public/assets/player and public/assets/furniture.

## Player

Create a production 2D chibi RPG player character sprite sheet. Young adult male trainee navigator, short BLACK hair, blue eyes, teal and charcoal sci-fi navigator uniform, silver shoulder trim, dark pants and compact boots. No backpack. Cute detailed anime pixel-art style with crisp dark outlines, large head about half of total height, consistent identity throughout. Exactly 12 full-body figures arranged in a strict 4 column by 3 row equal grid on a 1536x1024 canvas. Column 1 facing front/down, column 2 facing back/up, column 3 facing left, column 4 facing right. Row 1 idle feet together; row 2 walking left leg forward; row 3 walking right leg forward. Each figure completely contained in its own 384x341 cell with generous 25 pixel padding, equal scale, horizontally centered and feet baseline same relative height in every cell. Background must be a single SOLID vivid magenta #FF00FF for local chroma extraction; no magenta anywhere in clothing, no checkerboard, shadows, ground, labels, text or scenery. Full bodies including hair and boots must not touch or cross their cells.
